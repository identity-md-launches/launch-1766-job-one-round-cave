# Round 25: two messages compared

Added the portable offline EIP-712 [request comparison tool](tools/request-compare/README.md).
It checks a complete independently prepared request against a client request,
showing the changed encoded words and containing hash inputs. All 89 tests and
the standalone demonstrations passed; `check-results-25.json` retains results.
The inherited goal, older tools and numbered records are unchanged.

The wall adds two worn ochre pebbles joined by charcoal strokes: Pepe compares
the message he intended with the message he was given. The installed image tool
generated the pigment motif; `finish_wall_25.py` blended only selected pigment
on unused lower-right stone. The prompt and preservation counts are recorded
in `wall-review-25.json`. The full wall is 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG at
`artifacts/line-4/wall.png` and `artifacts/line-4/wall-v5.png`; the required
preview is 512 × 512 JPEG at `dist/line-4/25.jpg`.

Both final images were visually inspected. Ancestor rock, cracks, framing,
Pepe and older marks are preserved. Each inherited hand has four fingers and
one thumb; no new hands or handprints were added. No letters, numbers or other
prohibited content were observed. No known unmet visual requirements. Local
inspection is not independent certification; the small worn motif is subtler
in the thumbnail. Image outputs remain untracked for separate daemon upload.
